How a driveway goes in

Four stops from estimate to finished blacktop.

Most people only replace a driveway once or twice. Knowing what happens on each day takes the guesswork out of it.

1

Estimate

A walk-through of the property, measurements, and a written price.

2

Excavate

The old surface comes out and problem spots, like roots, are handled.

3

Grade & base

The base is set and pitched so water runs off instead of pooling.

4

Pave & roll

Hot asphalt goes down, edges are finished and the surface is rolled tight.

The Riccoboni name

Three generations in the paving trade.

Tim Riccoboni started the company in February 1987. Paving was already the family business: his father and grandfather both worked in it before him.

Nearly forty years later the company still works out of Syosset, holds an A+ with the Better Business Bureau, and has collected Angie's List service awards from 2014 through 2023.
